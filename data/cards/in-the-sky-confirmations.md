# In the Sky: confirmed card details

These reference notes supplement [the user-supplied parchment text](parchments-in-the-sky.txt). They are not an executable expansion catalog. Confirmation source: the user's message on 2026-09-08.

## Luxury farms

The user confirmed that neither card has a printed resource name. Use stable resource identities and descriptive display labels; do not present the labels as official names.

| Proposed resource ID | Descriptive label | Copies | Resource class | Confirmed placement restriction |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| `luxury_cloud` | Cloud wool | 1 | Luxury | Great Cloud board only. |
| `luxury_plains` | Bird | 1 | Luxury | Plain territories only. |

The user's descriptions were “like cloud wool(?)” and “like chocobo.” The second restriction maps to the existing `plains` terrain, not the carrot-producing `field` terrain. No additional board restriction was stated for that farm. General ownership and building-slot rules still apply to both farms.

Both resources are Luxury, not Wondrous. Their display labels may change without changing their identities, placement restrictions, or scoring category.

## Parchment classifications

The user confirmed that **Merchant's Signet** (written “Merchants Signet” in the source file) and **Cape of Dawn** are the two golden-shield Treasures. The other eight expansion parchments are Missions.

These classifications must be used by Nibblonacci and existing Treasure-counting and copying effects; variable scoring does not make these two cards Missions. The source text remains unmodified.

## Confirmed board and District rules

- The cloud board is transcribed and used in gameplay; review its [territories and adjacency](../../review/cloud/index.html). The user confirmed exactly four cloud corners on 2026-09-08: C1-1, C1-5, C5-1, C5-7; not C3-1 or C3-7.
- District history was confirmed on 2026-09-08: a territory that has ever belonged to a District disqualifies future Districts containing it from a new Coin reward. This includes Camps, Rainbow connections, later additions, and changes of ownership. Selected cards continue to resolve as one pick before rewards are checked.
